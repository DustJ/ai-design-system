# Logo Usage

Version: 0.1

## Primary Logo

**File:** `logos/svgs/emergent-software-logo.svg` (use on light backgrounds)
**File:** `logos/svgs/emergent-software-logo-white-text.svg` (use on dark backgrounds)

Use the primary logo whenever space allows. Minimum display height: **56px**.

## Stacked Logo

**File:** `logos/svgs/emergent-software-logo-stacked.svg` (use on light backgrounds when a square aspect ratio is required)
**File:** `logos/svgs/emergent-software-logo-stacked-white-text.svg` (use on dark backgrounds when a square aspect ratio is required)

## Single Color Logo

**File:** `logos/svgs/emergent-software-logo-full-white.svg` (use when full color isn't possible or on Brand Red background)

## Logomark Only

**File:** `logos/svgs/emergent-software-logomark-only.svg`

Use only when the full logo won't fit (e.g. favicons, app icons, social avatars).  
Minimum display width: **32px**.

---

## Clear Space

Always maintain clear space equal to the height of the logo's letterform on all four sides.  
Never place other elements, text, or imagery within this zone.

---

## Approved Backgrounds

| Background                 | Logo Version                                       |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| White `#FFFFFF`            | emergent-software-logo.svg                         |
| Light Cool Gray `#F0F4F7`  | emergent-software-logo.svg                         |
| Dark Cool Gray `#000C1A`   | emergent-software-logo-white-text.svg              |
| Photography / busy imagery | emergent-software-logo-white-text.svg with overlay |
| Brand Red `#D11C1A`        | emergent-software-logo-full-white.svg              |

---

## What Not To Do

- Do not stretch or distort the logo
- Do not recolor the logo outside approved palette
- Do not add shadows, outlines, or effects
- Do not place on low-contrast backgrounds
- Do not recreate the logo in text or other typefaces
