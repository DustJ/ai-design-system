# Emergent Software Boilerplate & Approved Copy

Pre-approved, voice-aligned copy for reuse across decks, one-pagers, proposals, email signatures, and web pages. Pull these as written, or adapt them within the rules in `voice.md`. The factual figures are fixed and must not be changed.

## About paragraph — extended (~100 words)

Emergent Software is a full-stack Microsoft technology partner that helps mid-market organizations grow through scalable software, cloud, and data solutions. Founded in 2015 and remote-first with engineers across the US and Latin America, Emergent brings genuine depth across software development, cloud platform, data and analytics, AI, security, and managed services, rather than specializing in one area and subcontracting the rest. Senior engineers lead and execute every engagement, and AI is embedded across the delivery process through proprietary agentic development tooling. With over 10 years of experience and five Microsoft Solutions Partner designations, Emergent supports the systems it builds long after go-live.
